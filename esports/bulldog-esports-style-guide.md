# Le Grand Bulldog Esports — Brand Style Guide

A quick reference for keeping thumbnails, banners, overlays, and anything
else on-brand and consistent with the team jersey.

---

## Color Palette

Sampled directly from the jersey and logo files, not eyeballed — these
are the actual colors already in production.

| Swatch | Name | Hex | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟩 | Forest Green (primary) | `#2E6822` | Jersey shoulders/sides, wordmark "LE GRAND" |
| 🟢 | Deep Green (tonal) | `#123816` | Ghosted chest/back graphic, halftone dot fade |
| 🟨 | Jersey Gold | `#E8C838` | Cuffs, side panels, nameplate "FULL NAME" text |
| 🟧 | Logo Orange | `#F89800` | Sticker-logo outline, mascot eyes |
| ⬛ | Black | `#0A0A0A` | Jersey body, text plates, backgrounds |
| ⬜ | White | `#FFFFFF` | Wordmarks, player nicknames/numbers |
| 🟫 | Slate (mascot head) | `#386860` | Sticker-logo bulldog head fill |

**Note on Gold vs. Orange:** the sleeve-patch logo uses a brighter orange
(`#F89800`) for its outline, while the jersey trim uses a slightly more
muted gold (`#E8C838`). Treat **gold as the primary accent** for new
materials (banners, thumbnails, overlays) since it's the color actually
worn on-body, and reserve the brighter orange for logo reproductions
specifically so the mark itself doesn't get redrawn in a slightly
different color.

## The Two Marks

Two bulldog marks are in play, with different origins and different
jobs. Don't mix them up.

1. **Esports Sticker Logo** (small/badge use)
   The teal-slate head with glowing orange eyes, bold black keyline,
   orange/gold outline — this is the *esports program's own* mark, used
   on the channel banner, sleeve patch, profile pictures, favicons, and
   thumbnail corner badges.
2. **Official School Mascot** (large surface texture)
   The gray bulldog with the green/yellow-spiked collar and red mouth —
   this is **Le Grand's official athletics mascot**, not an esports-only
   asset. It exists in two official forms: a full crouching body version
   and a head-only version (both shown in the reference art). The
   version ghosted across the jersey chest/back is this mascot traced
   down to green line art and faded tonally into black.
   Keep using it the same way: large, low-contrast, as background
   texture on banners/wide layouts — never shrunk to logo size, since
   it's meant to be *felt* more than read at that scale. Because it's
   the official school mark, keep its proportions and pose recognizable
   rather than redrawing it loosely — it's representing the whole
   school's identity, not just the esports team's.

Practical rule: **sticker logo = this team's mark, small and sharp.
School mascot = the whole school's mark, large and tonal.** That
pairing — a program-specific badge over a school-wide watermark — is
what makes the jersey read as "part of Le Grand athletics" rather than
a generic esports jersey with a dog on it.

## Typography

- **Display font:** bold, heavy-weight condensed sans, all caps
  (production reference used here: *Big Shoulders Bold*, a free
  equivalent to the jersey's condensed athletic lettering)
- Wordmarks split color mid-phrase: `LE GRAND` in green, `BULLDOG` in
  gold, `ESPORTS` in white — keep this two/three-tone split when
  reproducing the full lockup
- Player nameplates: `FULL NAME` in small gold caps above a large white
  `NICKNAME`, numbers in large white condensed numerals

---

## Texture & Layout Language

- **Halftone dots** — a gradient field of dots (solid → sparse) used as
  a fill transition, always in the tonal deep green, always fading
  *into* black rather than sitting at full density everywhere
- **Diagonal chevron cuts** — hard-edged angled color blocks (see cuffs,
  side panels) rather than curves or soft gradients
- **Black plates for text** — wordmarks sit on a solid black bar/shape
  instead of floating directly over background art
- Flat color fields with sharp keylines throughout — no soft drop
  shadows or glows, no gradient fills on shapes

the mascot in full color (e.g. school-wide material, not esports-team
specific), pull from the reference art rather than the green tonal
version, since the tonal version has already lost the gray/red detail.

## Quick Do's / Don'ts

**Do:** keep gold + green + black + white as the core palette; use the
sticker logo small, the tonal illustration large; keep type condensed,
bold, and all-caps.

**Don't:** introduce new accent colors (no blues/purples/neon — that's
generic-esports-template territory, see the DALL·E banner draft as the
example to avoid); soften edges with gradients or glows; shrink the
tonal illustration down to logo size.
